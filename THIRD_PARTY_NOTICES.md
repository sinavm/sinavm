## Inventory

A complete component-by-component inventory must be maintained as part of
release review. For each third-party component, record its name, version or
commit, source, copyright holder, license, and any required attribution,
source-offer, modification, or redistribution notices.

Do not treat this document as a claim that every dependency or bundled asset
has already been audited. Before redistributing a release, verify the actual
contents, dependency manifests, generated bundles, and applicable license
obligations, and add the required notices here or alongside the relevant
component.

## Reporting an omission

If you believe a third-party notice is missing or inaccurate, contact the
repository owner privately through the SiNAVM GitHub profile.
